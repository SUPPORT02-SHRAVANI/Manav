The two doctypes keep the same things in different fields. Each print format therefore only gathers the values of its doctype into one variable `q` and then includes this file, which draws the page from `q`. A change of the look is made here once and shows in both. It follows the design the user supplied on 2026-10-13 (file quotation_template (2).html, "Modern Minimalist Black & White"). That file was a sample with the figures typed in, styled by scripts loaded from the internet; here the same look is plain CSS and tables, because the PDF maker (wkhtmltopdf) runs no such scripts and knows neither flexbox nor grid. What `q` holds (every amount already as printable text; lists may be empty): company, date (a date or None), number customer_name, address (HTML), attn, phone, issuer_address (HTML) refs list of [name, value]: Ref. No., Ref Dt., Sales Engg., Cust ID. All four are given always; a value may be empty and is then printed as an empty field notes list of plain lines printed under the strip (the message lines of an old quotation) machines list of {code, name, image, spec_text, description, rate, qty, amount, options} image: address of the machine's picture, or empty spec_text: the specification lines, one per line; description: text or HTML options: list of {code, name, description, rate, qty, amount} loose_options options whose machine is not on the quotation, same keys as options subtotal, taxes (list of [label, amount]), grand_total payment list of HTML lines for "Payment Info" terms HTML: a table with one term to a row, or paragraphs / a list signer, designation Optional switches (2026-10-28, for "Manav Modern Quotation CIF"; a print format that does not give them prints as before): no_specs the "Equipment Specification" box of each machine is left out no_page_per_machine the machines follow one another instead of each starting a new page show_hsn the line tables get an HSN Code column, from the key "hsn" of each line no_tax_line no "Tax / Duties: As Applicable" row when there are no taxes grand_total_label the name of the grand total row (default "GRAND TOTAL") subtotal_label the name of the subtotal row (default "Subtotal"; added 2026-10-29) subtotal may be empty: its row is then left out. price_line, price_amount one bold line " ...... Working, top to bottom: 1. Head of every page, as on the old printed quotation: the letter head (the principal's header, see manav/manav/letter_heads.py), or the company name without one; the title QUOTATION; and the quotation box: the customer, its address, Kind Attn and phone on the left; quotation number, date, Ref. No., Ref Dt., Sales Engg. and Cust ID on the right. 2. (The quotation box is part of the head of every page, see 1.) 3. The notes, at the start of the first page. 4. For every machine: its "Equipment Specification" box (below), followed at once by its lines (see 5.), then the next machine. The box shows the machine's picture on its left when the Item has one. The specification lines are shown as small name / value cells, four to a row (three beside a picture): - a line "label : a || b" under a heading "#Title || S.A.E. || Metric" shows "a / b"; - under a heading whose second column is Units ("#Descriptions || Units || Model") it shows the value with its unit, "b a"; - a line "label : value" shows the value; a line without " : " is printed across the box; - a heading line starts a new group with its title above it. A machine without specification lines shows its description instead: as cells when it is written as a list ("Title; feature, feature" or six comma parts or more), each cell with a name worked out from its wording (see feature_rules below); as rows when it has several lines with "label : value"; otherwise as plain text. 5. Lines: under each machine's box, a line table of that machine and its options; after the last machine, the loose options. The machines are numbered 1, 2, 3 on a blue row; the options of machine 1 are 1.1, 1.2 ..., indented under it; loose options continue the machines' numbers. Each machine after the first (box and lines) starts a new page. There are none when the quotation has no lines (an old quotation loaded without its items). 6. Payment info on the left; on the right the totals: subtotal, taxes ("As Applicable" when there are none, as the machine quotations leave taxes to the terms), grand total. 7. The terms as one bordered table across the page (No. / Term / Details), right after the totals; it may go on over the next page between two terms, with its heading row repeated. Then the signing person. 8. The footer of the letter head, when it has one, at the foot of every page. The page is put out in two copies, one for printing from the browser (without the browser's own date, address and page number lines) and one for the PDF made on the server.
